# SyncHeroic privacy policy

SyncHeroic runs on your Android device. It has no SyncHeroic server, account,
analytics, advertising, telemetry, or crash-reporting service.

Your TrainHeroic email and password are encrypted with a key held by Android
Keystore. They are sent only to TrainHeroic to sign in. Workout data is read from
TrainHeroic and written to Health Connect on your device. SyncHeroic reads
exercise-session records to avoid duplicates and, when you allow it, to align a
workout with times recorded by another application.

If you explicitly enable remote configuration, SyncHeroic contacts the public
SyncHeroic GitHub repository to download data-mapping configuration. Workout data,
credentials, and device identifiers are never included in that request.

You can delete every Health Connect record written by SyncHeroic and erase local
credentials and settings from the Data and privacy screen. Uninstalling the app
also removes its local data.

SyncHeroic uses an undocumented TrainHeroic API which may change without notice.
Use of the application is at your own risk, including with respect to
TrainHeroic's terms of service.
